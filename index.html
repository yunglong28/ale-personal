Alessandro Y. Longo

Alessandro Y. Longo is a researcher, strategist, and cultural agitator based between Berlin and Turin. He’s a PhD candidate at DREST (Italian Doctoral School of Religious Studies), where he investigates the emergence of synthetic intimacy and the problem of AI personhood. Alessandro is the initiator and maintainer of REINCANTAMENTO, an independent research and development group exploring technology, and politics, and spirituality through the lens of re-enchantment. He has spent several years contributing to cooperative protocol design and blockchain governance through Curve Labs and Circles Coop.

currently

His latest essay, Mods of Production, was published with the Institute of Network Cultures. With Christopher Dake-Outhet he develops Minor Index, an experimental local AI initiative, and he is part of the Solidarity Economies Lab at transmediale 2027, which is building a time bank for artists.

extended

Before the doctorate, he completed an MA in Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge at the University of Bologna, with a thesis on club culture heritage and digital archives, and a BA in Philosophy at the University of Turin.

His installations, video essays, and lectures have been presented at MFRU (Maribor), MacTe (Termoli), Panke Gallery and SAVVY Contemporary (Berlin), Unsound (Krakow), HackThePromise (Basel), Baltan Laboratories (Eindhoven), NYU Design and Media (New York), Accademia Albertina (Turin), and NABA (Milan), with residencies and grants from 0xSalon / Trust, C/O Berlin, and the Serpentine’s Beyond Cultures of Ownership programme. He has covered CTM Festival and transmediale as a critic.

He has worked as user research and ecosystem lead for Shared Visions, as communications manager and research assistant for the ERC-funded BlockchainGov project at CNRS, and as lead researcher on the Circles universal basic income pilot in Berlin. Minor Index is supported by the KSM Art Grant.
